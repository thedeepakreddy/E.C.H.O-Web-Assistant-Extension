ECHO Online Privacy Notice

Effective October 1, 2026

ECHO is local-first. It has no developer analytics, advertising, or data-broker service. Cloud AI is disabled until you explicitly enable it in Settings.

What ECHO handles

Depending on the feature you choose, ECHO can handle your prompts, the current page URL and title, selected or relevant webpage text, visible form labels and non-sensitive field contents, tab information, tool results, conversations, preferences, memories, workflows, watchers, highlights, and locally indexed pages. Password, one-time-code, payment-card, and similarly sensitive input values are excluded from page reads.

API keys and connection settings you enter are stored in Chrome's local extension storage. That storage is not an encrypted password vault. Agent mode can instead use credentials managed by the local Echo Helper and connected services.

When data leaves the browser

When cloud AI is enabled and a request cannot be completed locally, ECHO sends your prompt and only the relevant context needed for that request to your selected provider: Anthropic, Google, Together AI, OpenRouter, or Groq. Web-search requests can also be processed by that provider and its search service. Those companies process data under their own terms and privacy notices.

If you connect email, GitHub, OpenClaw, or another agent app, requested data is sent directly between the local helper and that service. ECHO asks for approval immediately before external messages, posts, account changes, destructive actions, and payments. ECHO does not sell personal information or show targeted advertising.

Storage, retention, and control

Settings, chats, memories, cached answers, workflows, watchers, highlights, and the optional page index stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall ECHO. Remembering pages is off by default, requires a site allow-list entry, and excludes known private sites.

Private Agent Browsing uses an incognito window and ephemeral conversation scope. Its chat, URLs, actions, model conversation, and cache are not retained by ECHO after the task. Your cloud provider may retain requests under its own policy.

Settings lets you export non-secret data, forget sites, clear pages or cached answers, delete all ECHO data, and withdraw cloud consent. Exports exclude API keys, device tokens, and app credentials.

Permissions and security

ECHO uses webpage, tab, scripting, storage, alarm, notification, side-panel, context-menu, and optional local-helper capabilities to provide its features. Network requests use HTTPS or secure WebSockets where supported. Keep Chrome and ECHO updated and protect your operating-system account.

Children, changes, and contact

ECHO is not directed to children under 13. Material notice changes use a new consent version, disabling cloud AI until accepted. Questions and reports can be filed through the ECHO GitHub issue tracker.
